    An onsen village power puzzle

    High in the mountains, Hotarudani has three farmhouses, a tofu shop, hot springs — and no electricity. Build a grid that keeps every shoji glowing, even after the sun drops behind the ridge.

    Goal: grow to 70 residents, then keep every light on for a full day and night without burning fuel.

        How to play

        Light the village

        Put down a power source, then run utility poles to the buildings. A pole connects to anything within 3 tiles and lights every building within 2. Drag with the pole tool to lay a line.

        Lit buildings pay for the power they use. Dark buildings pay nothing.

        Then the sun drops behind the ridge

        Solar stops at 18:30, right when every shoji lights up. Batteries store the midday surplus. Fuel generators fill any gap, but fuel gets dearer every day and the smoke builds up. At 100 smog, the village office shuts the grid down.

        Wind (day 2) blows day and night, when it blows. A mizuguruma water wheel (day 3) and onsen wells on the hot springs (day 4) give steady power.

        Read ahead

        The Next 24 hours chart shows what's coming: clean supply, demand, and the amber battery line. Red means someone will be sitting in the dark. Next concern says what will go wrong first, and when.

        Each morning, Grandma Chiyo of the Kikuya Inn sends a letter about the night.
