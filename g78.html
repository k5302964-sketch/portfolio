A little sky-collecting game from the canals of Vlinderhaven

Swifthouse

On the roof of the skinniest house on the Kettlegracht, you fold swifts out of tin foil and fling them at the sky. Where they land, lost and peculiar strays are waiting to be carried home.

Raise the house floor by floor, until a swift can fly past the Topmost Bank of cloud.

How to play

Fling

A marker slides along the foil strip. Tap Fling! or press Space when it crosses the gold notch. A perfect throw goes farthest, but a wobbly throw still gets somewhere.

Catch

Where your swift lands, a few strays appear. Move the net with a short drag or the arrow keys, and hold it over one stray until the gold ring fills. The near one is a sure thing. The far one is usually rarer and worth more. Take your time: the clock starts after they appear.

Bring them home

Each stray moves into a room and earns tinkels while you play. Tap a room to meet its residents, change the wallpaper or move someone upstairs.

Build up

Fling power throws farther. A new floor lifts the roof, adds three places for strays and raises your launch height. A wider net is easier to aim and gives you more time. The next-throw preview at the top shows how far you can reach, and who lives there.
