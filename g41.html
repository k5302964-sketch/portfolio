How to play

WASD←↑↓→ Move JLeft click Quick swing KRight click Hold to aim a throw, release to let fly Space Roll (dodge through attacks) E Talk, open, rest C Charms   Esc Pause
  • Your swing smacks foes, smashes crocks and turf creels, and flips switches right beside you.
  • Your throw reaches far-away bells and levers — even over water and through railings. While aiming, a dotted line shows its flight.
  • Only brass walls with yellow-and-black stripes bounce the yoyo. Everything else just stops it.
  • Foes always warn you before they attack: a crouch, a raised pin, a puff, a !. Roll away, then strike back.
  • Knocked out? You wake at the room’s entrance with full health, minus a few shells. Everything you’ve opened stays open.
  • Beds and friendly glow-worms restore your health. Progress saves automatically.
