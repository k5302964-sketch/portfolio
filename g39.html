    Controls

    WASD / arrows Move Mouse Look Space Jump · wall-jump Shift / C Slide · crouch R Restart run Q Practice from last checkpoint Esc Pause

    Climb Jump at a ledge and keep moving forward. Tops up to about three metres above you can be climbed.

    Slide Hold slide while running. Sliding down slopes builds speed; jump out of a slide to keep it.

    Wall-run Jump alongside a yellow wall while moving forward and look along it. Press Space to kick off, then land on the next wall or ledge.

    The clock starts when you first move. A fall ends a timed run: press Space to go again, or Q to keep going from the last checkpoint as practice. Practice finishes still open the next course but do not set records. Tin sardines are hidden off the main line, and each miradouro (viewpoint) is worth stopping at for a moment.
