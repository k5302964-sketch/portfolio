OPTIONAL READING

How armour works

Penetration vs thickness

Every shell can pierce a certain thickness. If the steel in its path is thinner, it goes in and does full damage. If not, it stops.

Angles add steel

A sloped plate is thicker along the shell’s path. 100 mm at 60° is 200 mm in effect. Angle your hull to use this.

Ricochets

If a shell hits more than 70° away from square-on, it skims off and does nothing. It still bites if the shell is over three times wider than the plate is thick.

AP or HE?

AP pierces deep and hits hard, but can bounce or be blocked. HE barely pierces and never bounces. Even on thick armour its blast does some damage. Switching reloads the gun.

Components

Tracks soak hits but break, and you can’t move. Engine hits slow you, a jammed turret ring slows your turret, and a damaged gun reloads slowly. Parts fix themselves after a while. R uses one of two repair kits to fix everything fast.

Spotting

You see what your team has line of sight to. Juniper thickets and spruce hide you, but shells go straight through juniper. Firing gives your position away for a few seconds. Standing still keeps you hidden longer.
