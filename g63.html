A little light goes a long way.

Light every crystal. Tap a numbered socket to place a mirror, tap again to turn it, and once more to remove it. Large numbered controls do the same thing. On phones, open More controls to find them and choose a level.

LampSends a straight beam. Its direction stays fixed.
SocketThe only place for your mirrors. Numbers match the large socket controls.
CrystalGlows when light reaches it. A crystal absorbs the beam.
Fixed mirrorSilver, with two pins. Bounces light, but cannot be turned.
PrismSplits an incoming beam into two perpendicular branches.
WallStops light. Look for another route around it.

← / ↑ choose the previous numbered socket; → / ↓ choose the next. Enter or Space turns it. R resets. Tab reaches every button. Esc closes More controls.

There is no timer. Reset is always free. Every puzzle can be solved with the mirrors provided.
