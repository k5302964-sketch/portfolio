      The Olvari Harvest Festival

      The whole hill drinks ruby tonight

      Lanterns climb the lanes, the Bellflower Troupe plays until the stars come out, and every cup, bottle and jar on the long tables came from your pressworks. Nene Varta raises a glass to you from the front row.

        The festival is over, but the crossroads never sleeps: buyers keep coming, and there are corners still waiting for decorations.

        How to play

        Pluck, press, pour

        Pluck a pomegranate from Old Ruby, press it into cups of juice, and serve the customer waiting at the counter. Each cup earns zari.

        Spend zari on improvements. Each card says plainly what it changes. Helpers take over a job so you can watch the work flow, and every job stays yours to do by hand whenever you like.

        Buyers

        Now and then a buyer makes an offer. Accept it, ask for more (they may raise the price or walk away, and it never costs you anything), or decline. Load accepted orders from the crate.

        Grow upward and downward

        Open the vine-shaded courtyard, dig a bottling cellar, raise a rooftop dispatch terrace, then supply the Harvest Festival.
