01 / The spine

One gesture feeds three timescales.

The shot is immediate, investment spans attempts, and a new planet restarts the local climb.

The optimal timing target is easy to recognize. What remains difficult is stopping a moving needle, then understanding why a good swing did or did not become a good hole. The fan controls the initial shot; terrain, rebounds and hazards shape its outcome. The player spends the resulting coins on Strength, Speed and Bounce before trying again.

1 · TimeCommit the needle position; receive quality feedback.
2 · TravelWatch the trail and collision path change the shot.
3 · SettleHazard payout, resting position or completed hole.
4 · InvestChoose which launch property to strengthen.
5 · QualifyHole beyond the threshold unlocks the next reward.

Distance carries several meanings at once: current travel, personal record and the next hole target. Those meanings must remain distinguishable. An impressive flight ending in water can pay coins without completing the hole needed for a golfer unlock. This separates spectacle from progress, and makes the short game matter.

Flight is the reward and the explanation

The dotted yellow trail lets the player follow a fast, small ball across large changes of scale. Props and slopes give the flight local incidents; the space camera makes an upgrade feel larger than a percentage. A Perfect label describes the launch, rather than guaranteeing a safe landing.

Design interpretation. The game combines execution mastery with purchased power. If upgrades dominate every outcome, timing becomes ceremony; if collisions hide the cause of a loss, the Perfect label feels misleading. The adaptation must keep both the chosen launch and the visible collision history legible.

02 / Accuracy after spectacle

The hole makes the landing consequential.

A second precision task connects distance to a permanent unlock.

A ball resting on the green changes the interaction. The golfer reappears beside it, the cup stays visible and white dots show a short-shot cue. A checked sequence at 86 ft ends in a Birdie result. This is an accuracy phase after the large distance spectacle, rather than another full tee launch.

The cup is not just scenery. It closes the attempt, qualifies the distance for a collection gate and can turn a mediocre flight into a meaningful success. Direct sinks use larger celebrations such as Eagle and Albatross. Their exact classification and payout formula are not established by the available states.

Failure can be local

A missed short shot need not erase the launch that brought the player here. The supplied VIP account includes repeated green attempts, but the captured states do not establish the full retry entitlement or non-VIP policy. For the target game, retrying from the same green will be free and explicit; restarting the drive remains a separate choice.

The exact source gesture for this phase remains uncertain: the checked frames show a dotted cue and a committed ball, while no moving power slider is readable. The target will use a drag preview and release to commit, with cancellation before release. That is an explicit touch design decision.

03 / The investment carrier

A reward is useful when it changes the next shot.

The important economy question is what a coin buys in the next visible situation.

Strength, Speed and Bounce are visible investment categories, each with a percentage and next price. These labels promise three different ways to improve a flight. The underlying impulse, velocity and restitution formulas cannot be recovered from percentages alone.

In one checked purchase sequence, the wallet falls from 589 to 275 while the visible values advance from Strength 15% / 128, Speed 10% / 93 and Bounce 10% / 93 to Strength 20% / 176, Speed 15% / 128 and Bounce 15% / 128. The 314 difference equals those three prior prices. Subsequent Strength shows 25% / 241. This supports escalating prices and local 5 percentage-point steps, rather than an exact universal formula.

Coin gains feed shot power, while gems connect to wheel rewards and equipment offers. The purple Extra Ball explicitly advertises “Earnings and Power x3” for 6, 000 gems. That is a readable commercial promise; it is not a measured threefold physical outcome. A ball with a power perk changes the interpretation of every later distance record.

Adaptation consequence. Keep the earned coin → upgrade → farther hole chain. Replace ad multipliers, slot payouts and gem purchases with transparent earned progression. Random payout windfalls and a purchasable power multiplier would obscure whether the player learned the timing decision.

Core statements

  1. C1 · Timing changes the launch. With the same world and upgrades, the player stops a visible moving indicator; the chosen region changes launch quality and trajectory. Flight and collisions explain the eventual landing. Perfect remains a launch judgement.
  2. C2 · Investment changes the next experiment. A settled shot awards earned coins; choosing one of three upgrades changes a later flight property. Comparing attempts remains meaningful without buying power or relying on random jackpots.
  3. C3 · Hole completion qualifies distance. A long flight can stop in a hazard and pay without advancing a hole gate. A direct sink or successful short shot beyond the pending threshold advances collection; qualifying for travel leads to a special timed launch and a new environment.
