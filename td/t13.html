01 / The defining exchange

Destroy a rock.
Inherit its traffic.

The central decision is how long to stay under a target before its falling body—or its children—takes the same space.

Upward fire rewards alignment. Gravity and ground rebounds punish staying aligned too long. Breaking a large rock lowers one threat’s size while increasing the number of moving objects the player must track. Damage and safety therefore compete for the same input: lateral movement.

The damage–danger loop

1Align

Stand beneath a target to maintain a useful firing lane.

↓Damage

Reduce the rock’s remaining durability.

↔Split

A larger physical tier becomes several smaller hazards.

!Reposition

Cross away from the next ground contact and rebound.

↑Re-align

Recover firing time without surrendering escape space.

This is a causal model, not a numeric simulation: exact velocity, split impulse and damage formulas are not established by the available sequences.

What skill looks like

Watch the low arc, leave a lateral escape route, and return beneath a target after it passes. The player is allocating firing time between threats rather than manually aiming a barrel.

The cannon’s low position makes ground contact the danger boundary. Smaller fragments can be easy to destroy and difficult to notice. A busy shard or coin effect can briefly compete with a real hazard’s outline.

What progression changes

More firing capacity shortens a target’s life. That can open space sooner, but it also brings its split sooner. Stronger equipment changes the timing problem; it should not eliminate the need to read trajectories.

Scoring, physical tier and durability must remain distinct. A high score is a run outcome; a number on a rock belongs to that rock; a boss health bar belongs to the boss. Confusing these owners produces the wrong game.

02 / Entry and legibility

The first gesture teaches shooting.
The first rebound teaches survival.

“Swipe to SHOOT” makes entry immediate, but success also requires learning when to leave a firing lane.

The simple start screen asks for a gesture rather than a menu decision. The Playables variant also displays an active instruction to move, avoid and shoot. That text names both jobs, while the actual moving rock teaches their timing.

The strongest first lesson is a readable, survivable encounter: one large, low-durability rock, one clear split and enough space to step away. A good adaptation preserves that discovery and makes the dangerous transition easier to see.

Teach a behavior, not a button.

“Rocks bounce. Step aside, then keep shooting.” A short cue near the first rebound can connect motion with the required action.

03 / A failure-funded loop

Coins make another attempt
feel different.

A run can fail while the account advances. The next purchase changes the rate at which the player can solve the same spatial problem.

Attempt

Damage, dodge, collect. Risk is immediate.

Bank

Retain currency across attempts.

Choose

Spend on firing or earning capacity.

Return

Test the new timing against the same challenge.

Four upgrades, two horizons

Fire Speed changes projectile cadence. Fire Power changes damage capacity. Coins affects earning potential. Offline Earnings points to value between sessions. The four tab labels expose short-term survival versus longer-term earning decisions.

A first purchase is especially useful because its effect can be felt before the player understands an economy. Later tuning needs a controlled comparison: spending should reduce the time needed to break a matched target, without turning its fragments into unavoidable contact.

The footage shows a selected Offline Earnings value of 120%. It does not establish a payout rate, cap, clock policy or complete cost formula. Those are explicit balancing decisions for the target.

The economy should buy new timing opportunities, while movement remains the skill that converts them into survival.

Customization widens the horizon

The Castle milestone is labelled “Play 25 games” and shows 25/25. This gives repetition a visible account-level result beyond the current run.

The cannon catalogue introduces a second currency and a much longer savings horizon. Visible price labels span 100–1250 gems. The lowest displayed price exceeds the 55 gems in the observed boss payout, creating a gap between beating a boss and buying a new appearance.

That gap is a design tension, not evidence of a purchase requirement. The target keeps all combat progression free and offers immediate, understandable cosmetic progress.

04 / A destination changes the fight

Final ball, then a boss
worth staying under.

Ordinary levels resolve a finite wave. The boss keeps a valuable target high in the arena while numbered hazards continue to occupy the cannon’s lane.

“FINAL BALL” changes the player’s mental horizon: the remaining traffic can be finished instead of merely endured. The level bar and skull then point toward a different kind of encounter.

The large purple boss is visually distinct from faceted numbered rocks. In the checked encounter it shares the arena with ordinary hazards. The player must sustain a useful upward firing lane while preserving a route around what falls below it.

Victory changes the focus from avoidance to reward. A winged chest replaces the boss and later presents +55 pink gems. The visible score remains 610; that number is not a measured boss health pool.

The available run does not expose every later boss pattern or an ultimate campaign ending. The adaptation therefore specifies a complete repeating campaign rather than ending its game after this observed encounter.

Adaptation decision: freeze hazards during the reward.

A chest is an acknowledgement state. Give the player time to read its grant, save it exactly once, and choose the next level without an accidental collision.

05 / Ending without losing the account

A revive continues this run.
A retry starts another.

These actions have different promises. A clear mobile interface should say which world it will restore.

The Playables sequence gives a compact recovery example: the first round ends at score 6, a later round reaches 17, an Extra Life prompt holds that state, and resumed play continues from score 17 with the same coin bank. The next result records 19. The persistent bank connects rounds even when the run resets.

Keep the decision readable

The generic “Tap to continue” language does not itself certify the exact revive hit area, its cost or its safety grace. Those details need their own implementation contract.

The target gives two explicit controls: Revive once continues the current wave with a clearly communicated safe re-entry; Retry resets the attempt and score while preserving currency, purchases and unlocks. Neither asks for an advert or a purchase.

Make comparison honest

Another result shows score 143 at rank 463. The changing rank relationship does not establish a global ranking algorithm, a fixed cohort or fabricated opponents.

For an offline portable game, a local personal-best list provides a dependable comparison. A future real online leaderboard requires an actual service; invented global ranks add no trustworthy information.

06 / Readable arcade feel

Give every impact a job.

The source’s flat scenery, faceted rocks and large integer labels make a dense moving arena approachable.

Impact feedback should confirm damage without hiding the next collision. The visually useful hierarchy is: cannon location, low-arc hazards, durability labels, then particles and collected coins. Scene themes change mood while preserving that hierarchy.

The future audio brief separates information from excitement. Impacts and splits announce changes in the hazard field; collection confirms permanent value; a boss cue creates anticipation. Music can build momentum, but the whole loop must remain understandable in silence.

Three practical priorities

  1. Contact reads cleanly. Freeze or emphasize the actual cause of death; never imply that a coin or confetti killed the cannon.
  2. Split reads before spectacle. Distinct children emerge from a recognizable parent position. Debris fades before it can be mistaken for another numbered rock.
  3. Rewards leave the lane. Collection highlights move toward the bank; they must not resemble a falling damaging object.

The target sound and motion specifications are designed requirements. Original timbre, music, frame latency and exact collision response remain unverified.

Core statements to preserve

  1. Staying under a rock buys damage at the cost of collision exposure. If movement never changes damage opportunity, the core has been lost.
  2. Breaking a parent changes the hazard field. Physical splitting remains distinct from durability; small fragments remain relevant hazards.
  3. Earned currency changes a later attempt, while losing the attempt preserves the account. A retry must not erase purchases or double-credit old drops.
  4. A boss adds a sustained high target and patterned ground pressure. It must produce an observable change in aiming and dodging, rather than being an ordinary rock with a larger number.
  5. The loop can begin on the first gesture and resume quickly after failure. Teaching may clarify an action; it must not bury the arena in compulsory menus.

Bridge from sources to mobile web

Keep: one-axis positioning, upward fire, bouncing/splitting numbered threats, finite level resolution, persistent upgrades and a boss reward horizon. Translate: source menus into thumb-readable controls; ranks into honest local records; unclear continue language into explicit retry/revive; reported special objects into tested, labelled target rules. Improve: death explanation, first-rebound cue, safe chest state, readable damage/particle hierarchy. Omit from the baseline: ad interruptions, hearts sold as continues, modern drone/weapon/event systems and host-container chrome. Open tuning dimensions and their decision tests

Gravity, rebound energy, split impulse, hitboxes, projectile damage/cadence caps, scoring, wave completion accounting, earning curves, offline cap and boss patterns are design space. Select values using the defining exchange: players must be able to predict and escape a low arc, feel a firing upgrade against a matched target, identify a split, and reach another try without unexplained failure. Score by an explicit rule; never equate it with completion percentage or boss HP. Keep enough precision internally for upgraded damage even if integer labels are rounded for display.

Feel contract: launch and collision response should be immediate and stable across frame rates. Use a bounded fixed-step simulation with swept collision or sufficiently safe substeps; limit background catch-up. Test on a real mid-range phone before asserting performance. Tune rather than invent “source-accurate” milliseconds.

3. Touch, language, audio and state contracts

One finger, no invisible prerequisites

Ready → first pointer-down/drag starts the attempt and upward fire. Hold and move horizontally to steer; on release stop firing while the arena continues until pause, failure or clear. This release policy is Designed. Use a relative drag anchor so touching elsewhere does not teleport the cannon. Capture the initiating pointer; ignore extra fingers; handle pointer cancellation and lost capture without stuck firing. Clamp the cannon to a safe stage edge and keep the thumb’s tracking area below the principal hazard field.

Support touch and mouse; no hover, keyboard or precision double-tap requirement. Use a portrait logical playfield fitted into the browser with safe areas and readable top HUD; landscape gets a centered stage, never stretched collision geometry. Rotate/reflow while paused; require a deliberate input before resuming. Pause on page hiding and any platform pause reason. Clear held input, freeze timers and sound, and never fast-forward hazards when the tab returns. Use target controls of at least44px; exact sizing is a usability target, not a measured source value.

EN/RU is a target game requirement

Switch the complete player-facing interface, including tutorial cues, upgrade units/descriptions, boss rewards, results, pause, error/recovery copy and any future offer. Do not translate variable names or saved identifiers. Allow switching from settings during pause; return to the same state without restarting. Persist device language preference separately from progression; missing preference may use browser language. Test longer Russian labels at390px. This teardown remains English.

Independent Music and SFX

Provide two labelled0–100 sliders with numeric or percentage readouts and usable touch targets. A zero value mutes that bus alone. Persist each value; changing or muting music never changes effects and vice versa. Resume the audio context only after a user gesture. Platform global mute multiplies both bus gains to zero while preserving their saved slider values. Restore gains when unmuted; pause/hidden state suspends output and timers.

Designed sound roles: low-priority short firing pop with overlap/rate control; clearer impact acknowledgement; distinct split crack; light coin confirmation; separate gem/chest cue; a concise failure punctuation; boss anticipation and release cue. Use gain limits so rapid fire never overwhelms warnings. A calm, loopable plucked motif and light pulse can gain density in boss phases, ducking under critical effects. No claimed original instrumentation, BPM, native-music presence or source latency.

Persistent and temporary state

Separate account state (banks, bought upgrades, unlocked/equipped appearances, personal records), settings (language and audio) and attempt state (score, live entities, wave phase, revive-used flag). Retry creates a new attempt; revive restores the current one after safe re-entry. Resolve pickups, coin grants, level clears and chest grants once. Save after meaningful transactions; use versioned data, bounds checks and recoverable malformed-data handling. Storage failure keeps the current session playable and reports that progress may not persist. A failed read is not proof of an empty account.

Handle onPause reasons (hidden/signin/purchase/dialog/page) together with document visibility idempotently; do not auto-resume a user-paused game. Read audio.muted and handle onMute; global mute silences both saved slider settings without overwriting them.

Use the platform save API to reconcile local account data with authenticated data when available. Validate and merge monotonic earned/unlocked progression carefully; do not apply a monotonic max rule to language, equipped skin or audio settings. Guard local storage. A network/read error cannot erase confirmed progress or revoke an entitlement.

Measure questions, not arbitrary event volume

Use native analytics.track(event, props); do not add another analytics provider or duplicate automatic pageview/gameplay events. Emit on state changes. Use attempt IDs local to the session, selected variant/ruleset, level/cycle and numeric outcomes; exclude names, emails, raw pointer traces and exact device identifiers.

Publisher metadata plan

The future game.json should describe the actual finished game: title, honest hook/description, mood frantic, genre/action, realistic minutes, up to5 valid tags, players 1, coop false, inputs touch/mouse, controls with their actions, declared moments and the chosen accent. Do not claim gamepad, online ranks or an endless mode if they are not implemented.

Optional extras beyond the complete free baseline

Baseline: no purchase and no live products. Mountain/Desert/Castle, ordinary skins, upgrades, all levels, revive and every boss remain free. A possible later Night Observatory visual pack adds a finished theme and cosmetic cannon appearance; it changes no hitbox, damage, earning rate or access. This is a proposal, currently unavailable. Do not show an empty shop or a “Buy” button for it.

When that pack is complete and approved, declare its stable ID in game.json.products. Use monetization.getProducts(), purchase(id) and getEntitlements(). Show a buy control only for a sellable returned catalogue product, using its returned price. Owned state takes precedence even if the product is missing from the current catalogue. Failed catalogue/ownership reads cannot revoke a confirmed grant. Offline owned content stays available from verified saved ownership; an unknown offer stays unavailable. Apply cosmetics immediately after a confirmed grant, allow return to free visuals and preserve ownership on retry/reload. No fabricated prices, mocked success, compulsory ads or assumed player top-up/ad API.

Test catalogue absent, empty, error, sellable, already-owned, cancelled purchase, failed purchase, grant success and reload with confirmed entitlement before enabling an offer. Optional commerce must never block the first gesture or intercept a revive.
